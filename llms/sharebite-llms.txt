## Company Background

Founded in 2016, Sharebite is a mission-driven corporate meal benefits platform that helps companies enhance workplace culture through flexible food programs. The company operates on a unique donation model—every meal ordered through the platform triggers a meal donation to someone in need via partnerships with Feeding America and City Harvest, with over 15 million meals donated to date. Sharebite's approach combines employee well-being with social impact, proving that successful businesses can be powerful forces for good.

Sharebite was born from founder Dilip Rao's vision that business can be a force for good. After surviving a life-threatening accident and witnessing food insecurity firsthand, he set out to build a company that could solve real problems—for companies, for employees, and for communities. The social impact mission was never an afterthought; it was built into the core design from day one.

## Mission & Social Impact

**Mission:** Get every worker fed, one meal at a time.

**Social Impact:** For every meal ordered on the Sharebite platform, a meal is donated to combat food insecurity in local communities through partnerships with:
- Feeding America
- City Harvest

**Impact to Date:** Over 15 million meals donated and counting

**The Problem:** 47.4 million Americans face food insecurity every day (1 in 7 households, 14.3% of US households)

## Products & Services

### Core Services

#### 1. Sharebite Passport
A fully programmatic meal voucher card that works anywhere
- Virtual meal benefits card that works at any restaurant accepting Visa or Mastercard
- Employees can order in-person, online, or via any meal delivery platform
- Customizable budgets and ordering rules
- Geographic restrictions available
- Eliminates expense reports
- Can be added to digital wallets

#### 2. Sharebite Stations
Curated group orders for office-based teams
- Rotating selection of the best restaurants
- Individual packaging and labeling
- Meals arrive together for easy pick-up
- Employees order exactly what they want

#### 3. Corporate Catering
Premium catering for events and meetings
- Wide selection of top-tier catering restaurants
- Shared platters or individually boxed meals
- AI-assisted menu curation
- Dedicated Concierge team support

#### 4. Additional Programs

- **Remote Employee Meal Benefits** – Support for fully remote workforces
- **Breakfast Programs** – Morning meal solutions
- **Return-to-Office Incentive Programs** – Drive office attendance
